# Rusty Woods
Follow the east road out of Trash Town, past x=2400. Rusty Woods is part of the shared outdoor world, recommended for levels 5–10. There is no loading screen or separate solo instance. The town road now blends into a wider dirt approach before the forest becomes dense, so the region reads as one connected world instead of a hard map seam.

Moss waits at the trail camp (2470,760). Press E to accept Roots of Rust: land finishing blows on six forest creatures and the Ironroot Guardian, then return to Moss for a one-time reward of 200 Scrap, 200 XP and two medkits. The quest journal shows progress.

The existing combat archetypes and animations remain compatible with the realtime world, while names and health profiles make the encounters less repetitive. The Guardian uses a telegraphed ground slam and drops existing epic equipment. Ordinary enemies respawn after 25 seconds; the Guardian after 120 seconds. Finishing blows grant loot and quest credit, with no party reward duplication. Two extra salvage caches reset daily.
